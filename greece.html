About Greece

Greece is a beautiful country in southeastern Europe, famous for its ancient history, beautiful islands, Mediterranean beaches, delicious food and traditional villages.

A Little History

Greece is considered one of the important birthplaces of Western civilisation. Ancient Greece influenced philosophy, democracy, science, art and architecture. Visitors can still explore ancient temples, ruins and archaeological sites throughout the country.

Places to Visit

Santorini

Enjoy the famous white buildings, blue domes, beautiful sunsets and incredible views over the Aegean Sea.

Athens

Explore the Acropolis, ancient ruins, museums and lively streets filled with Greek culture and delicious food.

Greek Islands

Discover beautiful beaches, charming villages and peaceful island escapes surrounded by the Mediterranean Sea.
