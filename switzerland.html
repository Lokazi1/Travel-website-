About Switzerland

Switzerland is a beautiful country in central Europe known for its breathtaking Alps, crystal-clear lakes, charming villages, chocolate, cheese and scenic train journeys.

A Little History

Switzerland has a long history of independent communities and developed into a federation of cantons. The country is known around the world for its neutrality, beautiful landscapes and cultural diversity.

Places to Visit

Swiss Alps

Experience breathtaking mountain scenery, hiking trails, snowy peaks and unforgettable alpine adventures.

Lucerne

Visit this beautiful lakeside city and explore its historic streets, wooden bridges and spectacular mountain surroundings.

Zermatt

Discover a charming mountain village and enjoy incredible views of the famous Matterhorn.
