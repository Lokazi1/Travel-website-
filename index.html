🇹🇷 Discover Turkey

A country where Europe and Asia meet.

About Turkey

Turkey is a beautiful country located between Europe and Asia. It is famous for its rich history, delicious food, beautiful beaches, colourful markets and stunning landscapes.

History & Culture

Turkey has a long and fascinating history. Istanbul was once known as Constantinople and was an important city of the Byzantine and Ottoman Empires. Today, visitors can see ancient ruins, beautiful mosques, palaces and traditional markets.

Places to Visit

Istanbul

Visit the Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia, Grand Bazaar and enjoy the beautiful Bosphorus.

Cappadocia

Experience amazing rock formations and hot-air balloon rides over the beautiful landscape.

Antalya

Relax on beautiful beaches and explore the Mediterranean coastline.

🇬🇷 Discover Greece

Ancient history, beautiful islands and Mediterranean charm.

About Greece

Greece is located in southeastern Europe and is famous for its beautiful islands, beaches, ancient ruins, delicious food and traditional villages.

History & Culture

Greece has one of the world's most famous ancient cultures. Ancient Greece influenced philosophy, democracy, science, art and architecture. Visitors can still see temples and ancient ruins throughout the country.

Places to Visit

Santorini

Enjoy the famous white buildings, blue domes and incredible sunsets overlooking the Aegean Sea.

Athens

Explore the Acropolis, ancient temples and the historic centre of the city.

Greek Islands

Discover beautiful beaches, clear blue water and peaceful island villages.

🇨🇭 Discover Switzerland

Mountains, lakes, villages and unforgettable scenery.

About Switzerland

Switzerland is a country in central Europe known for its beautiful Alps, clear lakes, charming villages, chocolate, cheese and scenic train journeys.

History & Culture

Switzerland developed as a federation of different regions and communities. Today it is known for its cultural diversity, beautiful landscapes and famous tradition of neutrality.

Places to Visit

Swiss Alps

Enjoy breathtaking mountain views, hiking, skiing and beautiful alpine scenery.

Lucerne

Explore the beautiful lake, historic buildings and famous Chapel Bridge.

Zermatt

Visit this beautiful mountain village and enjoy views of the famous Matterhorn.

Greece

Relax in Santorini, enjoy stunning sunsets and classic Greek architecture.

Switzerland

Discover breathtaking Swiss Alps, beautiful lakes, charming villages, and unforgettable mountain views.
